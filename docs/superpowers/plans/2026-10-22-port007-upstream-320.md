## Review focus

1. **Timeline, a segment that enters and leaves DST while its two ends share an offset.** For example, a 9-month
   uncompressed segment in `America/New_York` from February to November. Upstream's daily probe finds both offsets; a port
   that probes only the ends finds one. Task 2 pins it (`dst-roundtrip-segment`).
2. **Timeline, the fall-back hour.** The repeated 01:00 in `America/New_York` on 2026-11-01 at a 15- or 30-minute interval
   must produce both ticks, and the skipped 02:00 on 2026-03-08 must produce none. Task 2 pins both
   (`dst-fall-back-hour`, `dst-spring-forward-hour`).
3. **Waterfall, absolute times far from zero with sub-unit spans.** For example, `start: 1700000000123.4` in `ms` with
   0.5 ms spans. `timeNumber` must take the `toPrecision` branch, and the shortest positive span (not the tick step) sets
   the resolution. Task 3 pins it (`epoch-offsets`).
4. **erd, a bridge refused by an unrelated route, and one by an earlier bridge.** Without both rows, a port that checks only
   entities (3.1.0 behaviour) passes every existing golden. Task 4 pins both, and an explicit `width: 4` on the crossing
   relationship (`erd-bridge-blocked-route`, `erd-bridge-blocked-bridge`, `erd-bridge-wide-route`).
5. **A 3.1.0 assumption baked into a test rather than into a fixture.** Hand-written expected strings, counts such as
   `JsTests:69`'s `> 250`, or the five-locale lists. Task 1's red list must be triaged into "fixture changed" and
   "hand-written expectation". The second kind needs a deliberate edit with the upstream line cited, never a bulk
   re-baseline.
