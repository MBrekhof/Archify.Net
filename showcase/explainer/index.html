Archify.Net explainer showcase

Four one-page explainers rendered by the Archify.Net port of archify 3.2.0 from their Markdown drafts (archify deliver explainer <case>.archify.md <case>.html --quality standard). Each page is the answer first, then structured panels with native diagrams.

  • First rentalChinese. A workflow diagram with linked steps, and a clause table. Draft: first-rental.archify.md
  • P99 tail latencyChinese. A before and after chart, and a workflow diagram of the triage order. Draft: p99-tail-latency.archify.md
  • TLS first visitEnglish. A sequence diagram of the first HTTPS handshake. Draft: tls-first-visit.archify.md
  • Finalize deliveryChinese. A workflow of the finalize gates. Draft: finalize-delivery.archify.md

Upstream's live copies: tt-a1i.github.io/archify/cases/explainer.
